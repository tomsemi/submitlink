# Route Boundaries

## Supported

- `directory listing`
- `request app`
- `claim listing`
- `account/profile` when it is a governed product-discovery surface

## Explicitly Authorized Editorial Pitches

Use `editorial pitch` only when the user has explicitly authorized article or
editorial submissions for the campaign. Each pitch must be free, narrowly
relevant, non-reciprocal, and addressed to a credible publication that accepts
unsolicited startup submissions. Keep it in a separate record from directory
listings, use factual non-promotional copy, and submit at most one pitch per
publication and editorial route.

Never treat a pitch receipt as article publication. Any resulting publication
requires a public URL before it can be recorded as published. Do not send cold
email, publish a guest post, accept payment, or agree to site modification as
part of this route.

## Separate Workflows Required

Do not execute articles, guest posts, press releases, community/forum/social posts, cold email, mass contact-form outreach, resource-page or partnership outreach, reciprocal-link outreach, paid-link acquisition, or blog-comment campaigns as directory-form work.

Record such a candidate as route-excluded and move it to a workflow with its own content, communication, publication, consent, and anti-spam controls. The explicitly authorized editorial-pitch route above is the sole exception; it still needs its own authorization and must not reuse directory authorization.
