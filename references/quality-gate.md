# Directory Quality Gate

## Purpose Boundary

Use this workflow for product discovery, accurate public product profiles, qualified referral traffic, and durable directory presence. Stop when the primary purpose is ranking manipulation, PageRank transfer, dofollow acquisition, anchor-text scaling, or backlink-volume growth.

Do not use DA, DR, dofollow availability, backlink count, or a paid "ranking package" as a positive selection signal.

## Required Per-Site Checks

Record each item before form work:

1. `Audience relevance`: `pass`, `fail`, or `unknown`.
2. `User/discovery value`: `pass`, `fail`, or `unknown`.
3. `Editorial/platform governance`: `pass`, `governed platform`, `fail`, or `unknown`.
4. `Paid/ranking-link offer`: `none`, `qualified paid placement`, `unqualified paid/ranking link`, or `unknown`.
5. `Directory/network quality`: `pass`, `fail`, or `unknown`.
6. `Automation/terms compatibility`: `allowed`, `manual-only`, `prohibited`, or `unknown`.
7. `Reciprocal link requirement`: `none`, `optional`, or `required`.

Set `Quality gate: passed` only when relevance and discovery value pass; governance is `pass` or `governed platform`; paid/ranking-link offer is `none` or `qualified paid placement`; network quality passes; reciprocal linking is not required; and the selected execution mode conforms to the site's terms. A required, truthful directory-verification badge may be an exception only when the operator grants standing or target-specific badge authorization and the campaign records `Badge verification exception: authorized`.

Mark the site `ineligible` with structured evidence for irrelevant sites, no genuine discovery value, unqualified paid/ranking links, forced reciprocal links without that explicit badge exception, low-quality/general-submit networks, empty or templated listing pages, or prohibited automation.

## Link Recording

After publication, record the actual anchor, href, rel, commercial relationship, and check time. Use brand/product/naked-URL anchors by default. Reject repeated commercial exact-match anchors. Require `sponsored` or `nofollow` when payment, a gift, a discount, or another incentive influenced placement.
